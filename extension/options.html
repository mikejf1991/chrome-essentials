    Make the group your own

    Drop in to save
    Drop a tab into a group and leave it there to save it. Passing over a group won’t add it. Rearranging saved tabs remembers their order.
    Pull out to keep browsing
    A tab you move out stays open. Its saved group replaces it with its last valid address.
    Keep your groups first
    Essentials stays first after pinned tabs. Your other saved groups follow, ahead of ordinary browsing.

    Leaving an allowed website moves that tab outside the group and restores its last valid address. Use Remove to stop saving one tab and leave its page open, or Close to stop the whole group intentionally. New tabs stay outside by default.
